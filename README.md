# AcewOnboard
web app for student onboarding and admission document system.
## ACEW Onboard: Innovation

ACEW Onboard is an intelligent admission-to-campus onboarding platform that combines document management, identity verification, fraud-risk detection, chatbot assistance and progress tracking in one simple web application.

Traditional admission systems mainly collect forms and documents. Students often face repeated data entry, unclear requirements, delayed verification and confusing correction processes. Admission officers, meanwhile, manually inspect documents and struggle to identify inconsistencies or possible impersonation.

ACEW Onboard solves this through a guided and explainable workflow. Students create one profile and receive a personalized checklist based on their programme, category, hostel preference and scholarship requirements. They upload documents once, while the system uses OCR, document-quality checks and cross-document comparison to identify issues such as blurry images, name mismatches, invalid dates or duplicate submissions.

The platform also includes a consent-based **Identity Check**. Students can complete liveness verification through a short camera interaction, followed by a 1:1 face cross-match with the photograph on their identity document. These features are used only as risk signals, not as automatic proof of identity or fraud. Uncertain cases are sent to an admission officer for manual review, ensuring fairness, transparency and an appeal path. Combining liveness with document verification helps address both static document manipulation and impersonation risks. [identomat](https://www.identomat.com/blog/how-liveness-detection-reduces-synthetic-identity-risk-in-digital-onboarding)

The unique innovation is the **ACEW Trust-to-Readiness Loop**:

```text
Profile → Documents → Trust Check → Human Review
        → Admission Tasks → Campus Readiness
```

Every task appears as a checkbox in the student dashboard. A circular **ACEW Readiness Score** shows the complete-process progress, such as “72% complete — 18 of 25 tasks finished.” The score updates only when real tasks are completed, including profile submission, document verification, identity review, fee payment, medical declaration and orientation registration. This makes progress clear and motivating without using childish points or leaderboards.

The integrated **CampusBuddy** chatbot provides personalized guidance using the student’s actual application status, pending documents, verification reasons, deadlines and next tasks. For example, it can explain why a marksheet was flagged, identify the next required action and respond in Tamil or English. It cannot approve documents, change admission status or invent institutional rules.

For admission staff, ACEW provides a review dashboard with extracted fields, document previews, risk reasons, identity-check results and audit history. Officers can approve, reject or request correction with mandatory explanations.

Therefore, ACEW Onboard’s innovation is not one isolated feature. It is the integration of **explainable AI, consent-based identity verification, human-in-the-loop fraud screening, chatbot support and checkbox-based campus readiness** into one student-centred workflow. The platform goes beyond solving admission delays: it improves trust, accessibility, data quality, student confidence and the transition from applicant to campus-ready student.
